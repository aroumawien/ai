01 • INTRODUCTION

Understanding Artificial Intelligence

Information Technology is defined by cycles of innovation, and few innovations have proven as far-reaching as Artificial Intelligence (AI).

AI refers to the branch of computer science concerned with building machines and systems capable of performing tasks that would ordinarily require human intelligence, such as reasoning, learning, perception, language understanding and decision-making.

AI has moved from a niche academic pursuit to one of the most transformative forces shaping Information Technology, the economy and society.

Objectives of the Assignment

  • Trace the historical origins of AI.
  • Examine the evolution of AI technologies.
  • Analyze AI as an emerging IT trend.
  • Evaluate opportunities and risks.
  • Examine the future direction of AI.

Scope and Methodology

The assignment adopts a descriptive and analytical research approach using secondary sources, including academic literature, industry reports and recent technology publications.

02 • HISTORY

The History of Artificial Intelligence

AI has developed through several important stages, from theoretical ideas about machine intelligence to today's generative and agentic systems.

1950

Alan Turing

Alan Turing published "Computing Machinery and Intelligence" and introduced the idea of a test for determining whether a machine could demonstrate intelligent behaviour.

1956

Dartmouth Workshop

The term Artificial Intelligence was formally introduced during the Dartmouth Summer Research Project on Artificial Intelligence.

1956–1980s

Symbolic AI

Early AI systems relied heavily on symbols, logical rules and explicitly programmed knowledge.

1980s

Expert Systems

Expert systems attempted to reproduce the decision-making abilities of specialists using knowledge bases and rules.

1997

Deep Blue

IBM's Deep Blue defeated world chess champion Garry Kasparov, becoming a major milestone in computer intelligence.

2012

AlexNet

AlexNet demonstrated the power of deep neural networks in image recognition and helped trigger the modern deep learning revolution.

2017

Transformer Architecture

The Transformer architecture became a major foundation for modern natural language processing and large language models.

2022

ChatGPT

ChatGPT brought generative AI into mainstream public use and significantly increased public awareness of large language models.

2025–2026

Agentic AI

AI systems increasingly move beyond simple question answering toward planning, tool use and execution of multi-step tasks.

03 • EVOLUTION

Evolution of AI Approaches

01

Symbolic AI → Statistical Learning

Early AI depended on explicitly programmed rules. Statistical machine learning changed this approach by allowing systems to learn patterns from data.

02

Shallow Learning → Deep Learning

Deep learning uses multi-layered neural networks capable of learning complex representations from large datasets.

03

Narrow AI → Foundation Models

Traditional AI systems were often designed for specific tasks. Foundation models can support many different applications.

04

Assistants → AI Agents

AI is moving from responding to individual prompts toward systems that can plan, use tools and perform multi-step tasks.

04 • CURRENT LANDSCAPE

AI Trends in IT, 2025–2026

AI is now one of the most influential emerging trends in Information Technology.

Generative AI

AI systems can generate text, images, audio, video and computer code.

Large Language Models

Large models support language understanding, generation, summarisation, coding and reasoning.

Agentic AI

AI agents can plan and execute multi-step tasks using software tools and external information.

AI Governance

Organizations are developing policies and frameworks to manage AI risks responsibly.

Smaller AI Models

Smaller specialized models can provide useful AI capabilities with lower computing requirements.

Multimodal AI

Modern AI systems increasingly work with text, images, audio and video.

Edge AI

AI can increasingly operate directly on phones, computers, sensors and other devices.

AI Infrastructure

GPUs, TPUs, cloud platforms and specialized AI accelerators support modern AI workloads.

08 • ASSIGNMENT NOTES

Research Summary

Historical Background

The history of AI can be divided into distinct eras, each characterized by a dominant technical approach, achievements and periods of reduced funding and interest known as AI winters.

Machine Learning

During the 1990s and 2000s, researchers increasingly used statistical machine learning, allowing systems to learn patterns directly from data. Decision trees, support vector machines and neural networks matured.

Deep Learning Revolution

The 2010s were driven by massive datasets, increased computing power, particularly GPUs, and improved deep learning algorithms.

Generative AI

Large language models based on Transformer technology demonstrated the ability to generate coherent text and code and later images, audio and video.

AI Governance and Ethics

Responsible AI development requires attention to fairness, transparency, privacy, safety, accountability and human oversight.

Conclusion

Artificial Intelligence has moved from philosophical speculation and symbolic logic through several boom-and-bust cycles to become one of the most influential trends in Information Technology.

IT professionals therefore need technical knowledge together with governance, ethical and critical-thinking skills.
